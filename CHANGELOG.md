# Changelog

## 1.0.6
- Bug fix: the scan radius is saved again (it was reset after a restart). It is now saved per character, like the other settings.

## 1.0.5
- Bug fix: no more extra "Ruins" pins on top of Fuling towers, villages and other places. Old extra pins are removed when you walk by.

## 1.0.4
- New resources: Vineberries, Fiddlehead, Smoke Puff, Kale Seeds, Flametal Deposit.
- New places: Infested Mine, Winding Tunnels, Morkhalla, Smouldering Tomb, Howling Cavern, Sealed Tower, Infested Citadel, Fader's Altar, Deep North Boss.
- New places: Hildir's Camp, Bog Witch Camp.
- New places: about 30 new kinds of ruins, camps and villages in every biome, including the Ashlands and the Deep North.
- New option: Replace location icons (traders and the sacrificial stones).
- UI change: settings are saved per character.
- UI change: new icons for Frost Cave, Sealed Tower and Charred Tower Ruins.
- Name change: Crypt is now Burial Chambers, Barley and Flax are now Wild Barley and Wild Flax.
- Name change: Farm Village is now two kinds: Draugr Village and Abandoned Farm.
- Bug fix: Fuling camps, villages and many other places were never pinned.
- Bug fix: Infested Mine, Meteorite and Dragon Egg were never pinned.
- Bug fix: hidden pins and settings were shared between characters.

## 1.0.3
- New icons for Frost Cave, Fuling Camp, Surtling, Trader and Infested Mine.
- New icons for Well, Dvergr Excavation, Infested Tree, Giant Remains and Viaduct.

## 1.0.2
- Bug fix: meteorite pins lost their icon after reloading the world.

## 1.0.1
- Bug fix: hidden pins and discovered kinds are no longer lost when a mod manager updates the mod.

## 1.0.0
- First stable version.

## 0.2.1
- New: works with Nav Compass - the pin name on the compass follows the label settings.

## 0.2.0
- New resources: seeds, wild barley, wild flax, wild beehives.
- New: one switch per dungeon kind, boss altars with trophy icons.
- New option: Replace boss icons.
- UI change: pins are collected even while hidden.
- Bug fix: untouched ore deposits are now pinned.

## 0.1.0
- First release: automatic pins for ores, berries, mushrooms, crops, herbs, beehives, dungeons, structures, portals and ships, with a settings window on the map.
